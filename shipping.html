FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SHIPPING FAQ

HOW MUCH DOES DELIVERY COST?

Delivery charges depend on your location and delivery option. The applicable delivery fee will be communicated when your order is confirmed.

CAN I CHANGE MY DELIVERY ADDRESS?

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change your delivery details. Address changes may not be possible once an order has been dispatched.

HOW CAN I CHECK MY ORDER?

Contact our customer care team with your order details and we'll help you check the status of your delivery.

WHAT IF MY ORDER IS DELAYED?

If your order has taken longer than the estimated delivery time, please contact us so we can check with the delivery service.
